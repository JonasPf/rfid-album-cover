ADR 0004: Sticker geometry

Status: accepted · 2026-10-14

Context

Stickers go on ISO ID-1 cards (85.6 × 54 mm, corner radius 3.18 mm). Cover art is square, so a 31.6 mm strip is left over. The user cuts with a paper trimmer, which only makes straight cuts.

Decision

  • Portrait layout: art on top, artist and title below. The 51 mm-wide text strip fits long titles in fewer lines than the 31.6 mm strip of landscape.
  • Square corners, with the sticker inset from the card edge by a configurable amount (default 1.5 mm).
  • A tight 3 × 3 grid with cut marks, so each trimmer cut separates two rows or columns.

Why 1.5 mm: a square corner inset by i sits 3.18 − √2 · (3.18 − i) mm inside the card's rounded corner. That is 0.10 mm at i = 1 and 0.80 mm at i = 1.5, which absorbs small cutting errors.

Alternatives

  • Landscape: narrower text strip, so more wrapping.
  • Rounded corners with gaps: needs scissors or a corner punch, and fewer stickers fit per sheet.
  • Exact size with bleed: any cutting error shows at the card edge.

Consequences

  • Every sticker has a thin white rim of card around it.
  • Users with a precise cutter can lower the inset. Below 0.93 mm, the corners overhang the card.
