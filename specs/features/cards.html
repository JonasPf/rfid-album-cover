Card list

The card entries waiting to be printed, each shown as a true-to-layout sticker preview.

Card entry

FieldMeaning
imageThe source image, kept in memory at full resolution.
cropA square region of the image, in source pixels.
artistFree text. May be empty.
titleFree text. May be empty.

Behaviour

  • Entries are listed in the order they were added. That order is also their order on the sheets.
  • Each entry shows a sticker preview, editable artist and title fields, and the actions Re-crop and Remove.
  • The preview updates as the user types, and when the inset changes.
  • The header shows the entry count and the sheet count, e.g. "11 cards · 2 sheets".
  • New entries slide into the list. Removed entries slide out.
  • If the list is not empty, leaving or reloading the page asks the browser to confirm first.
  • Previews need the bundled font. If it fails to load, the page shows "Could not load the font. Reload the page to try again."

Text fitting

  • The text area is the sticker width minus 3 mm padding on each side, and 31.6 mm minus 2.5 mm padding at top and bottom.
  • The artist goes above the title, both left-aligned. The block is centred vertically.
  • The title is bold and starts at 11 pt. The artist is regular, at 0.75 × the title size. Line height is 1.15 × the font size.
  • Lines wrap at spaces. If the block doesn't fit, the title size drops in 0.5 pt steps, down to 7 pt.
  • A single word wider than the line, or a block still too tall at 7 pt, means the text does not fit. It is then drawn at 7 pt, starting at the top of the text area so the artist stays visible, and clipped to the text area.
  • An empty field takes no space.

Warnings

Warnings appear on the entry. They don't block the PDF download.

WarningWhen
Text too longThe text does not fit (see above).
Low resolution (N dpi)The crop's side in pixels, divided by the art-area side in inches, is below 150.

Acceptance criteria

IDCriterion
AC-CARD-1With no entries, the list shows "No cards yet. Search for an album, upload an image or pick an emoji." and the PDF button is disabled.
AC-CARD-2Entries appear in the order added. The header shows the right card and sheet counts (9 stickers per sheet, rounded up).
AC-CARD-3Editing artist or title updates that entry's preview without a separate save step.
AC-CARD-4Re-crop opens the crop dialog with the entry's current crop. Confirming updates the preview.
AC-CARD-5Remove deletes the entry at once, with no confirmation. The counts update.
AC-CARD-6A short artist and title render with the title at 11 pt and no warning.
AC-CARD-7A title that needs a smaller size renders at the largest 0.5 pt step that fits, with no warning.
AC-CARD-8A title that does not fit at 7 pt shows "Text too long". It disappears once the text is shortened enough.
AC-CARD-9A crop of 250 × 250 px shows "Low resolution" at the default inset. A 1200 × 1200 px crop shows no warning.
AC-CARD-10With at least one entry, reloading the page triggers the browser's leave-page confirmation. With none, it doesn't.
