Search

Find albums in the iTunes catalogue and add them as card entries, with cover art, artist and title filled in.

Behaviour

  • The user types a search term and presses Enter or the Search button.
  • The page sends the request below and shows up to 25 albums. Each result shows a thumbnail, the artist and the album title.
  • Apple's album search misses some well-known albums that its song search finds, such as Nevermind by Nirvana. The request therefore asks for albums and songs, and each song stands for the album it is on. Results are grouped by album (collectionId) in API order: each album is listed once, where it first appears.
  • Clicking a result adds a card entry to the end of the card list. The result stays in the list, so the same album can be added again.
  • The card entry uses the 1200 × 1200 px artwork. The crop starts as the full square image.

Request

GET https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=<term>&media=music&entity=album,song&country=US&limit=200

200 is the API's maximum. Songs from the same album repeat, so the response usually still holds 25 or more distinct albums.

Response fieldUsed as
collectionIdGroups the album and song results of one album
collectionArtistName, else artistNameArtist. collectionArtistName is the album artist, e.g. "Various Artists" for a song on a compilation.
collectionNameTitle (for a song, the name of its album)
artworkUrl100Thumbnail; songs carry their album's art. Replacing 100x100bb with 1200x1200bb gives the full-size art.

Acceptance criteria

IDCriterion
AC-SEARCH-1Submitting a term (by Enter or the button) sends one request with the parameters above, with the term URL-encoded.
AC-SEARCH-2Each result shows its thumbnail, artist and album title. Albums appear in the order they first occur in the response, each once, and at most 25 are shown.
AC-SEARCH-3Clicking a result appends a card entry with the result's artist and album title, whose image comes from the 1200 px artwork URL.
AC-SEARCH-4Clicking the same result twice adds two card entries.
AC-SEARCH-5Zero results show "No albums found".
AC-SEARCH-6A network or HTTP error shows "Search failed. Check your connection and try again." Existing card entries are unaffected.
AC-SEARCH-7If the artwork fails to load after a click, no card entry is added and "Could not load cover art" is shown.
AC-SEARCH-8An empty or whitespace-only term sends no request.
AC-SEARCH-9An album that the response contains only through its songs, such as Nirvana's Nevermind, is listed with its album title and art. For a song on a compilation, the artist is the album artist.
